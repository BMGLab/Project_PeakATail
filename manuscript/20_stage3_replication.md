## Disclosure issues (verified; none change the counts)

1. **Top-gene names are unreliable in overlapping loci** — 33% of the top 30 gene rows name a
   neighbouring/readthrough model rather than the gene whose 3′ UTR holds the PAS (CD68 labelled
   SENP3-EIF4A1; PTPRCAP under CORO1B; FKBP11 under AC073610.2; CD68, PTPRCAP, FKBP11, GPX1, MDK,
   STARD10, ARPC1A have **zero** assigned PAS cohort-wide). Replication statistics are unaffected; the
   named top-switch list must not be published without re-assignment (tool issue #99 draft). Ambient-IG
   signature (IGLL5 in non-plasma pairs) noted. Overall context is sound: 62.5% of replicated PAS in
   own-gene 3′ UTRs, 96.8% in same-strand gene bodies.
2. **The MetBone exclusion stands (pre-registered) but its stated premise was wrong**: the 0.015%
   clip rate came from the caller sampling only the first 200,000 CB reads of a coordinate-sorted BAM
   (head of chr1). MetBone actually has mid-cohort clip evidence (306k molecules; 31.1% of its tested
   PAS with ≥2 own molecules). Never repeat the "no clip evidence" justification; primary and
   sensitivity agree to 0.4%. Tool issue: unrepresentative clip-rate sampling (issue #99 draft).
3. Cosmetic: per-sample manifests record `--threads 8` while the cohort caller used 16.
