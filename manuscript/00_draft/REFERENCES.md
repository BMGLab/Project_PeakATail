## Notes on this list

- **Year suffixes.** Two datasets are cited together with their source publication, as `CITATION_MAP.tsv` requires, and each pair shares a first author and year: Lukassen et al. 2018a is the *Scientific Data* descriptor and 2018b the GEO series GSE104556; Laughney et al. 2020a is the *Nature Medicine* paper and 2020b the GEO series GSE123904. If the journal's style excludes database accessions from the reference list, drop the `b` entries and keep the accessions in the Methods and Declarations prose, where both already appear (`CITATION_GAPS.md` G7.4).
- **Preprints.** Kaminow et al. 2021 (STARsolo) and Zhao and Rattray 2024 both remain preprints as of the 2026-09-02 verification pass and must be re-searched immediately before submission (`CITATION_GAPS.md` G4).
- **polyApipe.** Harrison et al. 2019 is an F1000Research poster with a Crossref DOI; polyApipe has no peer-reviewed paper. Read `CITATION_GAPS.md` G2 before shipping it.
- **Two citations remain unresolved and are still carried in `MAIN.md` as explicit, unresolved citation-token placeholders.** They are deliberately not closed here: the literature source of the spermatogenesis 3′UTR-shortening gene panel (`CITATION_GAPS.md` G1) and the data citation for the public Kinnex PBMC truth datasets (`CITATION_GAPS.md` G3). Nothing was invented to fill either.
