## (c) Abstract draft (≤200 words; bracketed = Stage-3 run pending)

> Alternative polyadenylation (APA) remodels 3'UTRs across cell types, and the poly(A)-site (PAS) signal is present in every 3' scRNA-seq dataset — but single-cell PAS calls are rarely benchmarked against independent truth, and on deep 10x data coverage-shape callers, including our own earlier version, emit call sets in which most sites lack atlas support. We present PeakATail, an aligner-agnostic framework that seeds PAS from non-templated poly(A) soft-clipped reads, reports clip-supported and coverage-only sites as explicit tiers with per-site molecule counts, filters internal priming, and tests cell-type APA switches with calibrated, replication-filtered statistics. Against curated PolyASite 2.0 point sites with gene-body-shuffled nulls (0.013–0.023), PeakATail's pre-registered default reaches atlas-agreement precision (100 bp) of 0.717 on PBMC and 0.741/0.755 on two testis replicates (gate ≥ 0.50) at detected-gene recall 0.17–0.20, below polyApipe's, and 79% of its PBMC sites lie within 25 bp of a PacBio Kinnex poly(A)-verified long-read 3' end (≥5 UMI); a six-tool benchmark shows that evidence type, not peak-calling sophistication, separates callers. A pre-registered "trusted novel PAS" definition missed its 70% long-read target (52%), reported as a negative result. PeakATail recovers the spermatocyte→round→elongating spermatid 3'UTR shortening gradient [ρ] and maps replicated per-cell-type switches across [17] lung-cancer samples. Open source (MIT): github.com/BMGLab/PeakATail.

## (e) Section-by-section outline

### Introduction (~5 paragraphs)
1. APA biology: 3'UTR isoform choice controls stability/localization/translation; remodeled in proliferation, cancer, immune activation.
2. 3' scRNA-seq reads pile near poly(A) sites — the signal is in every 10x dataset, but deep data also produces abundant coverage peaks that are *not* PAS, and single-cell PAS call sets are rarely validated against independent truth.
3. Existing single-cell APA tools (Sierra, scAPAtrap, SCAPTURE, polyApipe, scUTRquant, SCAPE, scDaPars, scTail, …) differ mainly in evidence type (coverage shape / poly(A) soft-clips / sequence models / catalogs); published benchmarks rarely use nulls, per-dataset denominators or long-read truth; differential tests are typically not calibrated and not replicated.
4. Gap → PeakATail: clip-seeded, tiered, IP-filtered PAS calling with per-site molecule support; calibrated and replication-filtered switch testing; a pre-registered, atlas-independent long-read test of de novo PAS (reported as a negative result); all within one aligner-agnostic, provenance-tracked framework with pre-registered gates.
5. Contributions summary: (i) precision-first PAS detection validated on two truth sets with nulls; (ii) a six-tool benchmark that reframes the field's accuracy claims; (iii) calibrated, replicated cell-type switches; (iv) UTR-length biology positive control; (v) a pre-registered long-read test of de novo PAS that missed its target, reported as a negative result; (vi) open source with full disclosure of the negative result that drove the redesign.

### Discussion
- What "trusted" means operationally and why precision-first with explicit tiers is the right default for a field where most published call sets have never been scored against a null.
- The benchmark lessons: evidence type over peak-calling sophistication; per-dataset reporting; atlas-only validation flatters internal-priming artefacts; F1 is truth-set-size dependent.
- The calibration lessons: pseudoreplication, double-dipping, dispersion floors, and the value of permutation nulls and replication filters (only one of six test configurations controlled FDR on correctly keyed data; `14`).
- The de novo lesson: an atlas-novel call set can be 70–250× enriched over a positional null and still miss an absolute long-read target (52% vs 70%); intronic sites and filter-missed internal priming dominate the residual, and any stricter definition chosen after seeing the data is exploratory until validated on held-out data (`16`).
- Interpretation of spermatogenesis and Laughney findings against the APA literature (scoped to what replicated).
- **Limitations (state plainly):** the clip-evidence recall ceiling (~29% of detected-gene atlas sites carry any clip read in the PBMC BAM; chemistry/aligner dependent — poly(A)-trimmed pipelines lose the evidence entirely) — the default's R_det is 0.171 (PBMC) / 0.202 / 0.205 (mice) and full-atlas recall 0.0865 / 0.0963 / 0.0980, below polyApipe's; **PeakATail is the heaviest tool in the panel by a wide margin: 280.1 GiB peak RSS and 3:45:53 wall on the 10k-cell PBMC BAM while using only ~1.4 cores despite `--threads 16`, i.e. a ≥300 GB node (2.1× the Stage-2 run, 2.8× the shipped caller; no other tool near 100 GB)**; the precision-first default and its gate were pre-registered (01:19) before the final run (02:59) but after a stale run failed the original gate and after a post-hoc sweep informed the ≥2-molecule threshold (disclosed, S9); "precision" is atlas-agreement at 100 bp, so atlas-novel true sites count against us (the Kinnex check: 0.786 of default PBMC sites within 25 bp of a long-read 3' end at ≥5 UMI, but only 0.521 of the atlas-novel subset — the pre-registered trusted-novel gate was missed, `16`); the final run's IP filter applied the forward-coordinate window on both strands (Stage 1d; found by the verifier, fixed on `fix/ip-filter-strand`, re-run planned after merge; estimated PBMC P@100 0.717 → ~0.706, gates unchanged) and its rule is looser than Kinnex's, so residual internal priming remains in the default; the switch-test calibration rests on one mouse and one tissue until the Laughney replication lands; the gate was met with a large margin but on three call sets from two datasets (one PBMC donor, one CellRanger BAM; the two mice are one study and chemistry); the PAS-profile clustering is an expression proxy, not isoform-choice discovery; Laughney samples are donor-correlated; no Read1 cleavage mode; the quarantined first-version Kinnex figure counted long-read support in records, not molecules (`16` counts UMIs and supersedes it).
- Future: Read1/TVN-priming cleavage mode, sequence-model scoring as a third evidence channel, spatial.

### Methods
1. Datasets & preprocessing (STARsolo/CellRanger as documented; whitelists; GEO/atlas accessions; Kinnex FLNC processing).
2. Read ingestion: UMI dedup, SAM-flag filtering (−F 3844), clip-site detection (`min_clip`, `min_purity`), wrong-end specificity control.
3. PAS calling: clip-seeded clustering (25 bp single-linkage, read-weighted mode, `--polya-min-reads` in distinct molecules), coverage-only tier-2, multi-PAS splitting; the legacy strategies as registry baselines.
4. Internal-priming filter: genomic A-tract definitions (−10..+30, ≥6 A or ≥70% A), `annotate` vs `filter` modes; the strand-symmetric window fix (`fix/ip-filter-strand`, Stage 1d) and `ema reannotate --genome-fasta` to recompute flags on pre-fix runs; the Kinnex-style rule (+1..+18, ≥12/18 A) as the planned `--ip-rule kinnex` option.
5. Annotation: tiered gene-end/3'UTR assignment (`max_gene_distance`, `utr_multiplier`, TIER_3 handling); note the TIER label reflects gene assignment, not 3'-end proximity.
6. Output tiers and the pre-registered default (`--peak-strategy clip_seeded --ip-filter --ip-filter-mode filter`, tier-1 ∩ IP-pass ∩ ≥2 distinct clip molecules; pre-registered `0e27b1a`, 2026-08-21 01:19, before the 02:59 run); `low_confidence` file; note that in `filter` mode `annotatedpas.bed` carries no per-site IP flag and that `pasbed.bed` drops zero-count gene-assigned PAS after the cell filter (0.45% PBMC, 3.7% mouse 1) — the scored set is the post-cell-filter set.
7. Differential APA: within-gene Fisher with `count_mode="cells"` and marker pre-selection off (`--marker-top-n 0`) as the reliable default (`14`); NB with shrinkage; permutation-calibrated q mandatory with marker pre-selection or `nb_pairwise`; Fisher gene denominator from the full matrix; replication filter; effect-size floor.
8. Length metrics: PDUI-classic (proximal/distal rule, strand-correct), proportion, entropy; structural direction; `switch trend`.
9. Trusted-novel PAS definition (pre-registered, `13` §3) and long-read validation protocol (Kinnex x3p / GEM-X truth, 25 bp strand-matched, UMI support tiers, gene-body-shuffled null, Wilson CI); outcome reported in `16` as a negative result.
10. Validation protocol: curated PolyASite 2.0 point sites (569,005; GRCh38.96), protein-coding TES, detected-gene denominators (285,136 PBMC / 126,686 testis), windows, gene-body-shuffled nulls (3 seeds), atlas-agreement precision / R_det / full-atlas recall / F1_det definitions, motif and IP analyses; atlas-free runs for scoring (no snap circularity); `LC_ALL=C` for every sort/bedtools step.
11. Benchmarking protocol: tool versions, parameters, environment fixes, hardware, resource measurement, `score_tool.py`.
12. PAS-profile clustering (TF-IDF/LSI/Leiden) as an optional utility, with the gene-sum ablation.
13. Reproducibility: frozen commit per run, `run_config.json`, content-hashed `run_manifest.json`, drop ledgers + survivor invariant, `reannotate` branching; code/data availability (MIT, GitHub + Zenodo DOI, all manifests as supplement).

## (f) Positioning vs existing single-cell APA tools (honest, reliability-centred)

**Defensible differentiators (each tied to evidence in this repo):**
1. **Evidence-tiered output with per-site molecule support.** Clip-supported vs coverage-only tiers, UMI-deduplicated clip counts in the BED score column, IP flag per site — users can see *why* a site was called. No compared tool exposes this.
2. **Pre-registered precision-first default and published gates.** The default output and its gates were fixed in writing before the final run (`0e27b1a` 01:19 vs arms 02:59, 2026-08-21) and the gate passed on all three call sets (P@100 0.717 / 0.741 / 0.755 vs ≥ 0.50); the stale run's failure and the post-hoc sweep that informed the threshold are disclosed (S9).
3. **Calibrated, replicated switch testing.** Null-calibration as a shipped check, permutation-q fallback, replication filter across samples, effect-size floor — a documented answer to pseudoreplication and double-dipping.
4. **Atlas-independent long-read test of de novo PAS** against a positional null — reported honestly as a negative result (52% vs the 70% target, `16`); de novo sites ship with molecule support, hexamer tier, feature class and IP status, not a "trusted" label.
5. **Benchmark methodology:** one scorer, curated point-site reference, per-dataset denominators, gene-body-shuffled nulls, replicate reproducibility, long-read re-ranking, the cleavage-offset finding, and the evidence-type result.
6. **Engineering/reproducibility layer:** aligner-agnostic input (STARsolo, CellRanger, Alevin-fry — three CellRanger-input bugs found and fixed, with a fixture in CI), `reannotate` branching, append-only drop ledgers with survivor invariant, content-hashed manifests, frozen commit per run.
7. **Optional utility:** PAS-profile clustering for label-free exploration when no GEX labels exist — explicitly an expression proxy (S4).

**What we must NOT claim:**
- **Accuracy leadership** beyond what the final-run gates show; never a single cross-dataset accuracy number; never F1 without the truth set named.
- **"Precision" without "atlas-agreement"** — every precision number is atlas-agreement precision at 100 bp against curated PolyASite 2.0 point sites; atlas-novel true sites count as false positives; the Kinnex long-read check (`16`) is the atlas-independent complement and is quoted alongside.
- **F1 superiority** — the default's F1_det lead over polyApipe (+0.015 PBMC / +0.010 mouse) is not a meaningful margin and scUTRquant's catalog F1 is higher; the claim is precision, not overall accuracy.
- **The non-IP PBMC ≥2-molecule file as the default** — `pas_tier1_ge2mol_noIP_POSTHOC.bed` (n 62,110, P 0.591) has no IP filter and is not the pre-registered default; the default is the IP-arm `pas_PRESPEC_precision_default.bed` (n 44,394, P 0.717).
- **"Trusted novel PAS" as a positive claim** — the pre-registered definition reached 52% (not 70%) Kinnex concordance at 25 bp (`16`); stricter variants were chosen after seeing the data and must be pre-registered and validated on held-out data before any de novo site is called "trusted". The funnel is reported as a negative result.
- **Recall parity** — the default's R_det is below polyApipe's (−14% PBMC, −19% mouse) and must be shown with full-atlas recall.
- **Reproducibility leadership** — the shipped caller's advantage is against polyApipe only; scAPAtrap and Sierra are above it.
- **Clustering novelty or "cell identity from PAS usage alone"** — the ablation shows gene totals carry the signal.
- **FDR-controlled discovery** from the shipped Fisher/NB q-values; only calibrated or permutation-calibrated q-values after the fix.
- **The retired dump-based precision figure**, anything against the 18.4M dump, or atlas snap-rate as precision (circularity).
- **Stale Stage-2 numbers** as results; **quarantined spermatogenesis/Kinnex numbers** until regenerated per `11_verifier_corrections.md`.
- **Monotone four-stage spermatogenesis shortening** — the SPG step is fragile; claim three stages.
- Read1 cleavage-site precision, sequence-model filtering, spatial support — roadmap items.

**Positioning sentence for the Introduction:** "Rather than asking how many poly(A) sites a single-cell caller can report, PeakATail asks how many can be trusted: it seeds sites from poly(A)-tail read evidence, labels every call with its evidence tier and molecule support, tests cell-type switches only where they are calibrated and replicated, and reports its novel sites with their long-read validation outcome, including the negative one — within a single aligner-agnostic, provenance-tracked framework whose acceptance gates were registered before the results existed."
